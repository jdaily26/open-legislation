# Terms for legislative counsel

State-neutral discussion language. The trigger is a discretionary local financial incentive. Validate local fiscal authority, public records rules and remedies in the adopting state before introduction.

## §1   Coverage

“Covered award” means a discretionary cash grant or negotiated tax rebate that a city or county offers for construction or expansion of a facility principally used to house and operate computer servers. This act applies to covered awards first approved on or after [effective date]. Awards available automatically by statute are outside its scope.

## §2   Public review

At least 30 days before voting on a covered award, the governing body shall publish the proposed award and agreement, the estimated public cost, and a summary residents can comment on. It shall hold a public hearing and publish its response to material comments before approval. A material reduction in benefits after notice requires renewed notice and review.

## §3   Agreement

Before approving the award, the governing body shall secure a signed agreement, contingent on approval, with the project owner. It shall identify affected residents, the documented local need, and at least one measurable benefit selected after public input. For each benefit, specify its value or quantity, responsible party, recipient, funding, delivery date, duration and verification record. Require a successor to assume outstanding obligations in writing before transfer.

## §4   Verification and remedy

Within 60 days of each award anniversary, and at each payment milestone, the owner shall submit delivery records. The local fiscal officer shall post amounts due and delivered, outstanding obligations and corrective action within 30 days of each annual report. A resident may report a missed obligation to the fiscal officer, who shall publish a response within 30 days. On material breach, the officer shall notify the owner in writing and allow 30 days to cure. If the breach continues, the locality shall suspend undisbursed awards and seek any recovery provided in the agreement. Each agreement shall secure obligations that survive the final award payment.

## §5   Administration

Before approval, the fiscal officer shall identify who will verify costs and service delivery, estimate monitoring costs and name the budget that covers them. The governing body may decline the award if verification or security is inadequate.

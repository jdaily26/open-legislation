# Model State Bill

Version 1.0

## School District AI Purchasing Reports Act

An Act requiring public school districts to report paid artificial intelligence tools and features to the state education agency.

Be it enacted by the Legislature of this State:

## Sec. 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the School District AI Purchasing Reports Act.

## Sec. 2. Definitions

(a) “District” means a public school district in this State.

(b) “AI tool” means a software system or identifiable software feature that uses an artificial intelligence model to generate content, predictions, recommendations, or decisions for instruction or district operations.

(c) “Covered purchase” means an AI tool acquired for use by a district or one of its schools through a paid license, subscription, pilot, reimbursement, or shared purchasing agreement. It includes an identifiable AI feature bundled in paid software whether the feature is enabled or disabled. A standalone tool used without charge or purchased personally without reimbursement falls outside this definition.

(d) “State education agency” means the state education department acting under the direction of the state superintendent or equivalent state officer.

## Sec. 3. District inventory and filing

(a) Each district shall keep an inventory of its covered purchases, including purchases made by its schools. The district superintendent or designee shall file one return with the state education agency within 30 calendar days after the end of each calendar quarter and provide the return to the district board for purchasing and renewal review.

(b) The return shall cover every covered purchase active at any time during the quarter and identify additions, renewals, changes, and retirements. Include tools acquired and retired within the same quarter.

(c) A district may certify its prior return when every required field, including payments and access status, remains unchanged and no purchase was added or retired during the quarter. A district shall file a zero return for a quarter with no covered purchases.

## Sec. 4. Reporting fields and spending

(a) The return shall identify the district, vendor, product, AI tool or feature, intended purpose, authorized user groups, access status, contract identifier and dates, and amount paid during the quarter. Authorized user groups shall be described by role or grade band.

(b) The district shall identify each separately priced AI payment. For an AI feature included in a paid software bundle, report the bundle payment and separately identify the AI portion if the invoice or contract identifies it. Record an unpriced AI portion as unknown and exclude it from the total for separately identified AI spending.

(c) Use a consistent contract identifier across filings and count each payment once. For a shared contract, report only the district’s own payment. Record zero for a quarter with no payment. A zero payment does not resolve an unknown AI portion of a bundle.

## Sec. 5. Privacy and records

(a) A return shall omit student and employee names, individual identifiers, prompts, and education or personnel records. The agency shall limit access to individual district returns to authorized users under applicable law and shall publish aggregate spending and filing coverage under section 6.

(b) A district shall keep the purchase records needed to substantiate its return under applicable records retention rules and provide relevant records to the agency on request, with personal information excluded. This Act leaves existing student privacy and procurement rules in effect.

## Sec. 6. Administration and public reporting

(a) Using funds made available for this purpose, the state education agency shall provide a standard spreadsheet, secure submission process, field definitions, and district support before the first reporting quarter. It shall make the prior return available for updates and allow a district to submit corrected entries.

(b) The agency shall check returns for missing and inconsistent fields, track late or missing returns, and publish filing coverage and aggregate identified AI spending for each quarter. Publish the number of reported bundles with unknown AI costs separately and describe material gaps.

(c) After four reporting quarters, the agency shall review the accuracy and burden of the filing process and report whether the information supports district renewal decisions. The review shall use a sample of purchase records and feedback from districts of different sizes.

## Sec. 7. Correction and follow-up

The agency shall notify a district of a missing or materially incomplete return and give it 30 calendar days after notice to file or correct it. The agency shall refer unresolved filings to the state superintendent for follow-up under ordinary supervisory authority.

## Sec. 8. Effective date

This Act takes effect on the 180th day after enactment. Reporting begins with the first full calendar quarter after that date and includes covered purchases already active during that quarter.

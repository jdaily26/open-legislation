# Talking points

School District AI Purchasing Reports Act · Bill v1.0

## Opening line

“Before a school board renews software, it should know which AI tools the district bought, who can use them, and what it paid.”

## What to emphasize

- **See the whole purchase.** The inventory includes paid pilots and AI features inside larger contracts, even if the feature is disabled. *(Sections 2–3)*
- **Report what the invoice supports.** Record the identifiable AI price and flag the bundled cost when the AI share is unknown. *(Section 4)*
- **Keep student records out.** The return uses roles or grade bands and omits names, prompts, and education records. *(Section 5)*
- **Make it easy to update.** Start with the previous return, use a standard spreadsheet, and give districts 30 days after each quarter to file. *(Sections 3 and 6)*

## Example story

“Imagine your school board is voting on two software renewals. One invoice shows $3,000 for a reading tool used in grades six through eight. The other shows $12,000 for a suite with a writing assistant turned off. The quarterly return gives the board both facts. It can see that the district paid $3,000 in identified AI costs, ask the vendor what the other feature costs, and decide what to renew.”

## Close with an ask

“At the next school board meeting, ask which paid AI tools are up for renewal, who can use them, and which costs the district can actually verify.”

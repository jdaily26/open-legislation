# Model State Bill Template

\[State\]  |  \[Legislative session\]  |  \[House or Senate Bill number\]

## \[Short Title\] Act

An Act relating to \[subject\] and establishing \[main requirement\].

Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of \[State\]:

## Sec. 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the \[Short Title\] Act.

## Sec. 2. Definitions

(a) “Administering agency” means \[full agency name\].

(b) “Covered activity” means \[activity and any threshold that determines coverage\].

(c) “Covered person” means \[person or entity responsible for the covered activity\].

## Sec. 3. Application

(a) This Act applies to a covered person that engages in a covered activity \[state connection or jurisdictional condition\].

(b) This Act exempts \[specific category\] when \[condition for exemption\].

## Sec. 4. Required action

(a) A covered person shall \[perform the required action\] no later than \[number\] \[calendar or business\] days after \[event that starts the duty\].

(b) The covered person shall satisfy this requirement by \[measurable standard\]. The covered person shall submit \[proof of completion\] to the administering agency by \[date or deadline\].

## Sec. 5. Records and notice

(a) A covered person shall retain \[specified records\] for \[period\] after \[event\] and provide them to the administering agency within \[period\] after a written request.

(b) The administering agency shall make \[public information\] available \[where and by when\]. The agency shall handle \[information requiring protection\] in accordance with \[identified law\].

## Sec. 6. Administration

(a) By \[date\], the administering agency shall make available the forms and instructions needed to comply with sections 4 and 5.

(b) The administering agency may adopt rules to carry out \[specified sections\] under \[applicable rulemaking authority\].

## Sec. 7. Enforcement and review

(a) The administering agency may investigate possible violations of sections \[sections that create enforceable duties\].

(b) When the administering agency determines that a violation has occurred, it shall give the covered person written notice identifying the provision violated, the facts supporting the determination, the action needed to correct it, and the deadline to request review.

(c) The covered person has \[period\] after service of the notice to correct the violation and may request review within \[period\] after service. \[Review body\] shall conduct the review under \[applicable procedure\].

(d) If the violation remains uncorrected after the correction period, the administering agency may impose \[specified remedy\] when \[condition for final action following any timely review\].

## Sec. 8. Reporting and evaluation

(a) By \[first report date\] and every \[interval\] thereafter, the administering agency shall report to \[recipient\] and publish a public version of the report.

(b) The report shall state \[number or share of covered persons meeting the requirement\], \[measure of the intended outcome\], and \[cost measure\]. It shall compare those results with \[baseline and measurement period\] and describe material gaps in the data.

## Sec. 9. Transition and effective date

(a) This Act takes effect on \[date\]. Sections 4 and 5 apply beginning \[compliance date\].

(b) A covered activity underway on the effective date is subject to sections 4 and 5 beginning \[date or specified event\].

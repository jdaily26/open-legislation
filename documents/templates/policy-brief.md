# Policy brief template

\[Working title\] · \[Jurisdiction\] · \[Version and date\]

## Decision

\[Who needs to decide what, by when? State the ask in one sentence.\]

## Problem

\[Who is affected? Name the gap in current practice and give one concrete example. Cite the evidence.\]

## Recommendation

\[What changes? Name the responsible body, the trigger, what happens, and how people can verify it.\]

## Cost and trade-offs

\[Separate one-time and recurring costs, who pays, and what is estimated. Name the strongest objection and a workable alternative.\]

## Delivery and proof

\[Who funds, runs, and checks the work? Name deadlines, records, and the remedy if a commitment is missed.\]

## Next decisions

\[Name the first decision, what needs review, and the people who can resolve it.\]

## Evidence

\[Link each material claim to a source. Mark estimates and worked examples.\]
